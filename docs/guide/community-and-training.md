# Training, events and community

The **Learn & Share** section of the sidebar collects learning material,
upcoming sessions and resources your organization recommends. Your admins
choose which of these pages appear.

## Training

**Training** opens **Learning & Development**, where courses are grouped into
**Learning Tracks**.

- Pick a track to see its courses. Each track shows the percentage you've
  completed, and the open track shows a count such as "2 of 5 courses
  completed".
- Click a course to open it and watch or read its material. Your progress is
  saved as you go.
- A dot next to each course shows whether it's completed, in progress or not
  started.
- **Cert** marks a course that leads to a certification.
- **Unlocks Access** marks a course you need to finish before certain access
  can be granted. Hover over it to see what it unlocks.

Use **Search tracks or courses...** to find something specific.

## Event Calendar

**Event Calendar** opens **Upcoming Events**: a month calendar and a list of
workshops, webinars, office hours and community meetups.

- Use the chips above the list to show only one type of event.
- Each event shows its date, time, length and location, with a button to join
  the meeting when it's online.
- **Subscribe to Calendar** downloads the upcoming events as a calendar file
  you can add to your own calendar app.

## Templates & Assets

**Templates & Assets** opens **Reusable Assets**, a shared library of design
patterns, templates and components other teams have published.

- Search by title, description or team, or narrow the list with **Filter by
  Tags**.
- To share one of your own, select **Submit Pattern** and fill in its title,
  description, your name and a link to its code.

## Community Links

**Community Links** collects the resources, tools and documentation your
organization recommends, grouped by category. Links open in a new tab. Admins
choose the links; if this page is empty, none have been added yet.
