# Getting started

{{brand_name}} opens on a chat with the assistant. Everything else is in the
sidebar on the left. You sign in with your usual Databricks account, so there
is no separate password.

## The sidebar

The sidebar groups pages by what you're doing. You only see the pages your
organization uses and your role allows, so yours may be shorter than this list.

| Section | Pages | What they're for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discover & Analyze | **Ask Anything**, **View & Search Catalog**, **Reports** | Chat with the assistant, browse and search the data catalog, and manage reports the assistant generates on a schedule. |
| Requests & Approvals | **My Requests**, **Pending Approvals** | Follow the requests you've made, and act on the ones waiting for you. |
| Learn & Share | **Training**, **Event Calendar**, **Templates & Assets**, **Community Links**, **Guide** | Learning tracks, upcoming sessions, reusable patterns, recommended links, and this guide. |
| Watch Tower | Governance tools | For governance and platform admins. See [Governance tools](admin/governance.md). |
| Control Tower | Admin, workflow and catalog tools | For admins. See the [admin overview](admin/index.md). |

Your organization may also add links to other apps, which open inside
{{brand_name}} or in a new tab.

Use the arrow at the top of the sidebar to collapse it to icons when you need
more room. Each section can be folded away by clicking its heading.

## The account menu

Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar to open the account menu:

- **Guide** opens this guide.
- **Release notes** shows what changed in each version, newest first. The
  current version number is shown next to it.
- **Send feedback** lets you send feedback, request a feature or report a bug.
  It goes straight to the {{brand_name}} admins.
- **Clear my data** deletes your saved conversations and personalized
  suggestions on every device you use. Your requests and approvals are not
  affected.

## Notifications

The bell at the bottom of the sidebar shows how many approvals are waiting for
you. Click it to go to **Pending Approvals**. See
[Approve or reject a request](requests-and-approvals.md#approve-or-reject-a-request).

## What you can see depends on your role

{{brand_name}} gives you a role based on the groups you belong to. Most people
are users. Admins also see the Watch Tower and Control Tower sections. Your
role never gives the assistant more access to data than you have yourself: it
works with your own permissions.
