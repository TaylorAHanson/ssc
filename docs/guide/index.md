# {{brand_name}} guide

{{brand_name}} is where you find data, ask questions about it, and request
access or resources, all in plain language. Requests that need sign-off go to
the right approvers, and you can follow every one from start to finish.

This guide explains how to use it. Use the search box and the page list on the
left to find a topic, and the outline on the right to jump within a page.

## Start here

- [Getting started](getting-started.md): a tour of the sidebar, the account
  menu and what you can see.
- [Using the assistant](using-the-assistant.md): how to ask questions, get
  answers about your data and make requests in chat.
- [Discover data](discover-data.md): browse business domains, search the
  catalog and check where data comes from.
- [Requests and approvals](requests-and-approvals.md): track your requests,
  and approve or reject the ones assigned to you.
- [Training, events and community](community-and-training.md): learning
  tracks, upcoming sessions and shared resources.

## For admins

If you look after {{brand_name}} for your organization, start with the
[admin overview](admin/index.md). It covers settings, roles, workflows and the
governance tools. The admin pages only describe screens that admins can see.

## Can't find something?

Some pages in this guide may not appear in your sidebar. Admins choose which
pages your organization uses, and some pages are only for certain roles. If
something you need is missing, ask your {{brand_name}} admin, or tell us with
**Send feedback** in the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar.
